The can is the kit

Everything else here is commodity. The oven-controlled oscillator is what you are buying: a crystal held at 70 °C so it stops caring what the room is doing, disciplined by the satellites so it stops caring what the crystal is doing either.

It draws more power warming up than running, and it will be warm to the touch forever. That is not a fault to design out. It is the entire mechanism.
